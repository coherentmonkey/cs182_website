By Paul Struble 2025-12-11 GPT
View on Ed Stem →
In this interaction, I use the ChatGPT “Study and Learn” mode (on GPT 5.1) to interact with the LLM about State Space Models (SSMs). The model walks me through the lecture notes (which I attach in the first message as PDFs) while testing my intuition/understanding along the way and adjusting its course/conversation according to my responses. In this document, I provide some observations/analysis about the model in the “Study and Learn” mode. Part of the goal was to see how the "Study and Learn" mode responds to different kinds of user responses and how it would adjust its responses when I showed more or less confidence in my understanding.
