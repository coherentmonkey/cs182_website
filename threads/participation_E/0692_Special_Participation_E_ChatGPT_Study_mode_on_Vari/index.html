Special Participation E: ChatGPT Study mode on Variational Autoencoders

By Ayush Goel 2025-12-08 GPT
View on Ed Stem →
I used ChatGPT's study mode to understand variational autoencoders better. I have attached the annotated pdf of the transcript. Some notable things in my interaction: 1. I think GPT was able to provide very good intuitions for variational autoencoders, including why we need VAEs in the first place, the reparameterization trick and the different terms in the loss function. 2. I asked GPT to ask me questions to cross check my understanding. For the most part, it picked great questions and was able to adapt them based on my confusions and doubts. For example, I asked it a question about how the KL divergence term doesn't collapse the latent distribution, and GPT asked me a question regarding reweighting terms in the loss function to make sure I understood the tradeoff. 3. Sometimes, GPT started asking the same question repeatedly in different wording, especially towards the end when I had mostly understood the concept. In these scenarios, I had to guide the conversation myself with other questions I was curious about / details I didn't understand. 4. In the beginning, GPT provided too much information at once, trying to explain every detail in one shot. While the flow of ideas was logical and it started with the intuition, it still would've been better to break it up into sections and only move on until I understood each idea fully.
