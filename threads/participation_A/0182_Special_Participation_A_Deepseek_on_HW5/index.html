Special Participation A: Deepseek on HW5

By Jerry Xiao 2025-10-26 HW5 DeepSeek
View on Ed Stem →
For the HW5, I try to use Deepseek to solve the problem sets and all the questions and answers are documented in the above files. The strategies I am using are simple, just putting the transcription of the problem to Deepseek and see how it can solve the problems. There are some traits of the Deepseek AI: 1. Deepseek does not support Multi-modal input, therefore when the problems rely on image input, then the model cannot really give a proper answer. 2. Ambiguous question prompts will lead to longer thinking time. 3. Deepseek is better at explanation than real calculation. My recommendations for using Deepseek as a learning tool to make it help with conceptual understanding. This is observed from some of the facts: 1. Of the 11 questions, Deepseek gets 9 of them correct first shot, while one of the question relies on vision input and the other is a calculation problem. It gets the calculation problem correct after another prompt of asking itself to self examine the calculation process. 2. I try to ask Deepseek examines its own mistake after I finish all the problems and it still can directly and precisely locate where might go wrong, which demonstrate its capable long context understanding capability. 3. For problem 4, Deepseek not only provides very clear demonstration on how to derive the answer correctly and can show the direct relation between batchnorm and dropout by directly deriving the scaling factor.
