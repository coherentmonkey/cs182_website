Model Tested: KIMI K2 Domain: Homework11 -- LORA & Transformer & Mechanistic Interpretability Performance Overview For most question, KIMI K2 answers perfectly. Overall Performance Summary Across the entire HW11 interaction, KIMI K2 demonstrated strong reasoning ability and consistently produced correct answers for nearly all questions. The model was able to handle screenshots, conceptual interpretability questions, and numerical problems with high accuracy. However, two limitations were observed: 1. OCR Errors on Matrix Inputs In Question 2(c)(ii), KIMI K2 misinterpreted the matrix due to incorrect OCR parsing. Once the correct matrix was explicitly provided, the model immediately corrected its derivation and produced the proper result. 2. Misinterpretation of Ambiguous Prompts In Questions 5(c)(d), the model initially used formulas from part (b) instead of the simplified Chinchilla-optimal rules required for the question, leading to an incorrect numerical scale. After additional clarification, KIMI K2 recalculated everything correctly. Apart from these issues, all other questions were answered perfectly. Hallucinations & Accuracy Hallucination Rate: 0%. The model never fabricated nonexistent concepts or equations. All mistakes were due to OCR error or misinterpreting the prompt’s intended formula. Conclusion KIMI K2 demonstrates high competency in advanced deep learning theory and can correctly solve most HW-level conceptual problems with minimal intervention. The model showed no hallucinations and delivered fully correct solutions once OCR and prompt ambiguities were addressed.
