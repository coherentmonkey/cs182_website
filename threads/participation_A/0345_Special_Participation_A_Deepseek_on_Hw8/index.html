Special Participation A: Deepseek on Hw8

By Zesheng Cai 2025-11-28 HW8 DeepSeek
View on Ed Stem →
For HW8, I first provided Deepseek with a set of instructions to encourage step-by-step reasoning and self-verification. For each problem, I supplied both the image of the question and a direct copy-and-paste text version. After observing its behavior throughout the assignment, I summarized several notable characteristics: 1. Its self-checking mechanism was largely ineffective. Although Deepseek always performed a “self-examination” step, it rarely identified actual mistakes. Most of the time, it simply reiterated the correctness of its own answer rather than performing a thorough or systematic review of potential oversights. 2. It occasionally misinterprets small details in the prompt, though its overall understanding remains accurate. These misunderstandings were usually minor (e.g., subtle assumptions or edge cases), but they indicate that Deepseek may overlook fine-grained nuances in certain problem statements. 3. Its reasoning and explanatory abilities are very strong. Deepseek is well-suited for explaining solution steps and clarifying concepts. Since HW8 did not contain many heavy calculations, its computational accuracy appeared reliable in this context. 4. Its conversational and correction capabilities are adequate, but its error-localization ability is weak. When its solution was incorrect, Deepseek could fix the issue once I explicitly pointed out the problem. However, it generally struggled to independently locate the exact source of the error without guidance. Overall, Deepseek performs well as a conceptual explanation tool but still requires human intervention for precise error detection and critical verification.
