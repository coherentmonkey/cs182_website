Here is the online link: https://chat.deepseek.com/share/hcxrv1b7tn9s8c3lo0 Here is my annotated version of the log: https://drive.google.com/file/d/18ZU3GgmdtP_u84GnVxof8Kh2yrbpOotN/view?usp=sharing Executive Summary: From my observations, at least for DeepSeek, the model tends to capture most of the details of the problem-solving process within its internal reasoning. However, even after some targeted prompt engineering, it often fails to provide a detailed explanation of its reasoning and solution in the actual response to the user. At times, it even skips crucial steps in deriving mathematical expressions. For example, when asked for the minimum norm solution of ridge regression, it outputs the correct expression but provides little explanation of how it was derived. Most of the time, the model answers in a single step rather than engaging in a back-and-forth process to verify whether its solution is fully correct. This one-shot approach can be acceptable for straightforward problems, but it does cause errors in more complex cases. This issue is visible later in the annotated log, where the model fails to consider multiple possible cases for a single question. On the positive side, I did not notice any hallucinations so far.
