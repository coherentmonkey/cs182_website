If blocked, paste the exact sub-question into Gemini. 2) Compare & probe. Check Gemini’s output against my derivation; ask for clarifications or for the missing step. 3) Tighten. Clarify equation statements, symbol mismatches, and ask for the key identity or rules explicitly. Specific Example (Analogy + Clarification): Topic: Interpreting pθ​:X→Δ(X) in Q4. My Ask: “What does the arrow to Δ(X) mean in pθ​:X→Δ(X)?” Gemini’s Analogy (helpful): Sound equalizer. pθ​ = the equalizer; θ = slider positions; X = the set of audible frequencies; Δ(X) = all possible sound profiles (distributions over X). Changing θ selects a particular distribution in Δ(X). My Follow-up (to make it precise): “Please restate without analogy: define X, Δ(X), and what an ‘element’ of Δ(X) is.” Gemini (clarified): X is the sample space; Δ(X) is the set of all probability measures on X; for each θ, pθ​(⋅)∈Δ(X). Pattern used: take the first pass (analogy/intuition), then demand the formal mapping with symbols. Observations: Uses analogies to simplify (e.g., equalizer for pθ​:X→Δ(X)). Helpful as a first pass. Inconsistent symbols at times, likely from generic training patterns; needs nudging to match the homework question’s notation. Positives (What Worked Well): One-shot on standard rewrites: Correctly gave correct answers straight from around 30% of the questions Right methodology even when imperfect: When wrong/incomplete, it still pointed in the correct direction Negatives (Limitations and Effort Required): Notation slippage: Misread or drifted symbols Over/under-explanation: Wrapped easy steps in prose but skimmed tough steps unless I asked “elaborate on this step.” Prompting Strategies That Helped: “Derive further.” Ask for the exact algebraic substitution (e.g., show ∇p=p∇logp). “Elaborate / Why this?” Request the chain-rule Jacobian or the gradient wrt input vs. parameters explicitly. “Is my thought correct?
