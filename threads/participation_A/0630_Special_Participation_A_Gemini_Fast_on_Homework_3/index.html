Special Participation A: Gemini Fast on Homework 3

By Nazar Ospanov 2025-12-08 HW3 Gemini
View on Ed Stem →
I used Gemini Fast to complete the written questions for Homework 3. As in earlier evaluations, I instructed the model to restate each question, give a step-by-step solution, and identify uncertainties. I prompted each question separately to avoid context-window issues. The full annotated trace is shown in my notes . Overall, Gemini Fast performed well. It solved most derivations on the first attempt, produced clean LaTeX, and often gave explanations as good as or better than the staff solution. However, I observed two consistent weaknesses: Incorrect assumptions leading to wrong solutions. Gemini sometimes committed early to an interpretation that wasn’t implied by the problem, and the resulting chain-of-thought led to confident but incorrect answers (e.g., Question 5). Hallucination on research-figure interpretation (Question 3). When asked to analyze specific figures and tables from Tensor Programs V and the Spectral Condition paper, Gemini did not answer the actual sub-questions. Instead, it produced generic summaries of the figures and ignored the prompts, showing that Gemini Fast does not reliably ground its answers in visual research content. Thus, Gemini Fast is excellent for computational and mathematical deep-learning questions, but less reliable for conceptual reasoning that depends on precise assumptions or figure-based interpretation.
