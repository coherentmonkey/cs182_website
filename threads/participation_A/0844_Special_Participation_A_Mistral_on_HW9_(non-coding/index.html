Special Participation A: Mistral on HW9 (non-coding)

By Subhash Prasad 2025-12-11 HW9 Mistral
View on Ed Stem →
I used Mistral Le Chat on HW9 (non-coding), and it achieved 99% accuracy, solving everything correctly on the first try with only one minor notation error. This was done on the second prompt, as the first one tried to be a pedagogical aide by providing explanations without complete answers. Prompts Prompt 1: "Please complete all of the problems in this homework assignment." Mistral tried to be pedagogical, encouraging me to work through problems myself Prompt 2: "Give me the detailed answers and explanations. I need an answer key." This resulted in complete solutions with full derivations. Annotated Conversation https://drive.google.com/file/d/11VThgTMnqTfB7DuIIoTBzNxp0MFyCaiN/view?usp=sharing Strengths Zero arithmetic errors across dozens of calculations Perfect PyTorch einsum notation Explained why answers are correct, not just what No hallucinations or fake math Errors Used "bnd" instead of "bnk" in one complexity expression Conceptual understanding was clearly correct from explanation Explanations were lacking in the output from the second prompt, but this might be because I told the model to just give me the answers Thoughts The model's initial refusal to just give answers was interesting, as it wanted to be a tutor, not a solution key. This required explicit instruction to override. Without overriding it, the tool is well-formed as a place for students to close the gaps in their understanding after having attempted the problems by themselves.
