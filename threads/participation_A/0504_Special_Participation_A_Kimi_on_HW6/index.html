Summary of Kimi Performance on HW6 I tested Kimi AI’s ability to solve problems from hw6 focusing on GNN architectures. Overall, Kimi demonstrated strong reasoning capability across most questions. It correctly handled: Linear algebraic interpretations of GNN message passing Inductive proofs involving adjacency matrix powers Identification of valid permutation-invariant update rules Loss computation using masked training nodes Interpretation of max aggregation Analysis of computational scaling in GraphNet architectures For these, Kimi’s reasoning was correct and aligned with the solutions. Weaknesses Tends to produce over-engineered answers when a simpler one is expected Occasionally misidentifies structural details (e.g., graph neighbors) Does not always verify provided diagrams or datasets before proceeding The following LaTeX document compiles and evaluates Kimi’s responses from the linked chat session.
