Special Participation B: Opus 4.5 on HW03

By John Wang 2025-12-08 HW3 Claude
View on Ed Stem →
For the coding side of HW3, I worked with Claude 4.5 Opus inside the Claude Code environment to complete the µP visualization notebook. What I found most helpful was how quickly we were able to turn the abstract idea of “maximal update parameterization” into concrete, runnable PyTorch code. Opus not only filled in the TODO blocks for the µP optimizer and per-layer scaling, but also helped adapt the notebook to my Apple Silicon setup (M3 Max with MPS), fixing device issues, NumPy–tensor conversions, and SVD support by selectively moving computations to CPU. The interaction made the stability story behind µP very tangible: by comparing standard Adam to the µP-style updates across different widths, I could see in the plots how a single learning rate stops working under standard parameterization but transfers cleanly under µP. There were a few places where Opus proposed competing “reasonable” scaling rules (fan-in vs. fan-out), and I had to resolve them using the HW3 notes and the resulting curves.
